The Joy of Reading

A small page about big stories

Why read?

Books let you travel to other places, meet people you would never otherwise meet, and learn at your own pace. Even 20 minutes a day adds up to dozens of books a year.

Habits that help

  • Keep a book with you for waiting time.
  • Read at the same hour every day.
  • Stop books you do not enjoy and pick another.
  • Note down new words and ideas as you go.
